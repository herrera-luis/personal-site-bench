MDX is Markdown with the power of components. It means a prose-first authoring
experience without giving up interactive or reusable building blocks.

## The content collection

Each article on this site lives in its **own folder**, so its images and assets
travel with it. A typed schema validates the frontmatter at build time.

That's it — a small idea that scales nicely as the blog grows.
