Static-first doesn't mean static-only. It means defaulting to HTML and CSS,
and reaching for JavaScript only when an interaction genuinely needs it.

## The payoff

- Pages render instantly from a CDN.
- There's far less to break in production.
- SEO and accessibility come almost for free.
