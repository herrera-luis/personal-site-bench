Welcome to my new personal site, built with **Astro + MDX** and served as a
fully static site.

## Why a bilingual blog?

I want to write for both English- and Spanish-speaking readers without forcing
anyone to read a machine translation. Every article lives in its own folder and
can have an English and a Spanish version linked together.
