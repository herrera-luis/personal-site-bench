## Importing components

The `wide` frontmatter flag widened the reading column for this page, and the
figure above is an imported, colocated asset. Components let an article vary its
layout without touching any global code.

<Callout type="note" title="Frontmatter controls">

  frontmatter to change how an article is presented and listed.

## Why folder-per-article?

Each article is one folder with a file per locale. Adding a new article is just
creating a folder — no code changes, no central registry to update.

> Content first. The shell stays out of the way.

That is the whole idea: restrained design, readable typography, and content that
works everywhere.
