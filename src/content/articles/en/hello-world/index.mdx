Welcome to my personal site. This is the very first article, written in English.

## Why a bilingual site?

I write in both **English** and **Spanish**, so every article can have a
counterpart in the other language. Use the language switcher in the header to
jump between them.

- Built with Astro + MDX
- Static output, deployed to Vercel
- Per-locale RSS and a multilingual sitemap
