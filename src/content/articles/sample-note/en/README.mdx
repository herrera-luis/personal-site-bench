Durable pages do not need to be loud. They need a clear address, readable text, and links that keep working when scripts are unavailable.

<Callout title="Design principle">
  This article is rendered from MDX, but the navigation, language switcher, and prose all work as plain HTML.
</Callout>

The rest is restraint: a small set of routes, localized metadata, and a content folder that makes adding another article predictable.
