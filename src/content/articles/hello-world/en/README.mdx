This is the English edition of the sample article. It is written in **MDX**, so
it can mix long-form prose with imported components. Everything on this page is
rendered at build time and works with JavaScript disabled.

<Callout title="Why this article exists">
  It demonstrates the folder-per-article content model, the article reading
  view (H1 title, H3 dek, meta line, prose body), and a shared component
  imported directly into MDX.
</Callout>

## A section heading

Reading pages use a serif type scale and a restrained palette that follows your
system's light or dark preference. There are no downloaded web fonts.

- Add an article by creating one folder with `en/README.mdx` and `es/README.mdx`.
- Colocate images and assets next to the content.
- Frontmatter controls (`wide`, `layoutVariant`) let a piece vary its layout.

That's the whole loop: content-first, bilingual, and static.
