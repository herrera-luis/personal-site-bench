This is the first article on **Personal Site** — a deliberately small place for
long-form writing in two languages. It exists to prove that the template works
end to end: typography, localization, feeds, and embedded components.

<Callout title="Why this exists">
  Every article lives in a single folder with one MDX file per locale. Adding a
  new piece never requires touching the routing code.
</Callout>

## Writing without distraction

The reading view uses a system font stack, a restrained palette, and respects
your `prefers-color-scheme` setting. No web fonts are downloaded and no client
JavaScript is required to read or navigate.

### Custom structure per article

Frontmatter controls such as `layoutVariant` and `showToc` let an individual
article opt into a wider column or a table of contents without affecting the
rest of the site. This article sets `layoutVariant: 'wide'` and `showToc: true`.

## Two languages, one source of truth

Each article has an English and a Spanish counterpart. The language switcher in
the header preserves your place: switch from this page and you land on the
Spanish version of the very same article.
